# Phase 15 — Deferred Items & Guardrails

## Deferred
- Cloud hosting.
- Automatic cloud backups.
- WhatsApp/SMS.
- Payment gateways.
- GST automation.
- Double-entry accounting.
- Bank/cloud accounting integration.
- Report export/PDF.
- Major UI redesign.
- New business workflows.

## Guardrails
- Do not remove legacy tables without explicit approval.
- Do not introduce a second source of truth for income, expenses or reports.
- Do not move authoritative calculations to React.
- Do not weaken RBAC for convenience.
- Do not store secrets in source control.
- Do not test destructive database recovery operations against production data.
