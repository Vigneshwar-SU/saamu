# Phase 11 — Deferred Items & Guardrails

## Deferred
The following are not part of Phase 11 unless already implemented by an earlier completed phase:

- PDF salary slips.
- WhatsApp/SMS bill delivery.
- Payment gateways.
- GST.
- Double-entry accounting.
- Automatic income records generated from payments.
- Other future accounting automation.

## Guardrails
- Do not duplicate Phase 7 settlement functionality.
- Do not rewrite finalized payroll.
- Do not silently delete payment history.
- Do not trust frontend calculations for financial authority.
- Do not introduce SQLite.
- Do not add cloud dependencies.
- Do not implement later-phase functionality merely because it is convenient during Phase 11.
