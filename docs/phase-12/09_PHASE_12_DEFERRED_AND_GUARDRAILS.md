## Guardrails
1. Do not modify Phase 11 billing behavior.
2. Expenses and customer payments are separate concepts.
3. Do not automatically create income records from customer payments.
4. Financial calculations and permissions are backend-authoritative.
5. Preserve accurate historical expense records.
6. Do not create duplicate financial systems.
